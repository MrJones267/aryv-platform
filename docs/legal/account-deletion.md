# Delete your ARYV account

**Last updated: 23 August 2026**

> This page exists to satisfy Google Play's User Data policy, which requires a
> publicly reachable URL where anyone can request account deletion — including
> people who have already uninstalled the app. It must be published at a stable
> address (`https://aryv-app.com/delete-account` is the one referenced from the
> app) and linked from the Play Console listing.

You can delete your ARYV account and personal data in two ways.

## In the app

1. Open ARYV and sign in.
2. Go to **Settings → Privacy**.
3. Tap **Delete My Account**.
4. Confirm with your password, or by signing in again with Google if that is
   how you created the account.

Deletion takes effect immediately. You will be signed out on all devices.

## By email

If you no longer have the app installed, email **hello@aryv-app.com** from the
address registered to your account, with the subject line **Account deletion
request**. We will verify that the request comes from the account holder and
complete the deletion within **30 days**.

Include your registered phone number if you have it, so we can locate the
account.

---

## What is deleted

The following are erased permanently:

- Your name, email address and phone number
- Your profile photo, date of birth and address
- Your emergency contacts
- Your saved preferences, language and currency settings
- Your identity and vehicle verification documents
- Your authentication credentials and active sessions

## What is kept, and why

Completed trips, deliveries and payment records are **retained in anonymised
form**. Once your account is deleted, these records no longer contain anything
that identifies you.

They cannot be removed entirely for two reasons:

- They are also the **other party's** record of a transaction they took part
  in — a driver's earnings history, or a sender's proof of delivery.
- We are required to retain financial records for tax and accounting purposes.

Anonymised transaction records are retained for **[CONFIRM: 7 years]**.

## This cannot be undone

Deletion is permanent. Your ride history, ratings, saved places and any
remaining wallet balance are lost. If you want to use ARYV again afterwards,
you will need to create a new account and, if you drive or deliver, complete
verification again.

**Withdraw any wallet balance before deleting your account.**
