Post the trip you're already making, choose who comes along, and share the cost of the road with people going the same way.

Why drive

Your car, your trip, your rules

ARYV is not a taxi service. You drive where you were going anyway, and the empty seats help cover the cost.

  • Post a trip with your stops, seats and price per seat.
  • See every booking request, and accept or decline it.
  • Passengers pay you in cash. You both confirm the payment in the app, so each of you has a record.
  • Riders rate you, and you rate them.
  • Carry a package on a trip you're already making, for the price the sender names.
What you need

Three documents and a roadworthy car

Anyone can ride with ARYV. You only verify your documents if you want to drive or deliver.

  • You are 18 or older.
  • You hold a valid driving licence.
  • Your vehicle is roadworthy, registered and insured.

Documents you upload in the app

  1. Driving licence, in date
  2. Vehicle registration
  3. Insurance certificate, in date

ARYV staff review each one before you can offer rides.

How to start

From sign-up to your first passenger

  1. Get the appCreate your account. You can book rides straight away.
  2. Upload your documentsAdd photos of your licence, vehicle registration and insurance.
  3. ARYV reviews themStaff check each document. Once they are approved you can offer rides.
  4. Post your tripAdd your stops, seats and price, then accept the passengers you want.
Money

How ARYV earns

Passengers pay you, in cash. ARYV charges you a commission on what you earn through the app, and the app shows what you owe.

  • Settle your commission when ARYV asks you to.
  • If what you owe goes over your account's limit, you can't take new cash bookings until it is settled.
  • Trips arranged through ARYV must be completed through ARYV. Taking a passenger off the app to avoid commission breaks the Terms of Service.
Safety

Tools on your side too

The same safety tools protect drivers and passengers.

You choose who rides

Nobody joins your trip until you accept their request.

Pickup PIN

Your passenger reads you their PIN at pickup, so you know you have the right person.

A photo from today

You and your passenger each take a face photo for the trip, shown to the other and deleted a day later.

Emergency alert

An SOS button shares your location with your emergency contacts and ARYV's on-call staff.

Ratings both ways

Riders rate you after the trip, and you rate them, so everyone builds a track record.

Block and report

Block anyone you don't want to carry again, and report behaviour for ARYV to review.

These tools reduce risk; they do not remove it. In an emergency, contact the emergency services first.

Questions

Driving with ARYV

More in the Terms of Service, or write to hello@aryv-app.com.

How much should I charge per seat?

You decide. Passengers see your price before they book, and the price they see is the price they pay you.

How many passengers can I take?

You choose how many seats to offer, but never more passengers than your vehicle is licensed to carry.

Can I cancel a trip?

Yes. Either side may cancel before the ride. Cancel as early as you can so your passengers can make other plans.

Do I need to verify to ride as a passenger?

No. Documents are only needed to drive or to carry packages.
