How it works

From search to drop-off in four steps

Simple for passengers, in control for drivers.

  1. Search or postPassengers search for a ride. Drivers post the trip they are making.
  2. Request a seatThe driver sees your request and accepts or declines it.
  3. Meet and checkRead your pickup PIN to the driver so you both know it's the right car.
  4. Travel and payPay the driver in cash, then rate each other when the ride ends.
Safety

Built so strangers can trust each other

Sharing a car or handing over a package only works when both sides know who they are dealing with.

Verified drivers and couriers

Nobody can offer a ride or carry a package until ARYV staff have reviewed their identity and vehicle documents.

Pickup PIN

Each booking has a PIN that only the passenger sees. The driver checks it at pickup.

A photo from today

Passenger and driver each take a face photo for the trip, shown to the other once the booking is confirmed and deleted a day later.

Emergency alert

An SOS button shares your location with your emergency contacts and with ARYV's on-call staff.

Delivery code

A package is only marked delivered when the courier enters the code the recipient was sent.

Block and report

Block anyone you do not want to deal with again, and report behaviour for ARYV to review.

These tools reduce risk; they do not remove it. In an emergency, contact the emergency services first.

Help

Questions, answered

Still stuck? Write to hello@aryv-app.com.

How do I pay for a ride or delivery?

In cash, directly to the person: the passenger pays the driver, and the recipient or sender pays the courier. Both sides confirm the payment in the app so each has a record. ARYV does not hold or transfer the money.

Who sets the price?

For rides, the driver sets a price per seat, and the price you see when you book is the price you pay. For deliveries, the sender names the price and a courier who accepts agrees to it.

How are drivers and couriers checked?

Before anyone can offer a ride or carry a package, ARYV staff review their identity documents and, for drivers, their licence, vehicle registration and insurance.

What is the pickup PIN?

Every booking has a PIN that only the passenger sees. Read it to your driver at pickup so you both know you have the right car and the right person.

Can I cancel a booking?

Yes. Either side may cancel before the ride. Please cancel as early as you can so the other person can make other plans.

Are packages insured?

No. ARYV does not insure packages, so don't send cash or anything you can't afford to lose. If something goes wrong, report it in the app and ARYV will review the delivery.

What happens to my trip chat?

A ride's chat is deleted 24 hours after the ride is completed, unless the people in the chat vote to keep it.

Who can use ARYV?

Anyone aged 18 or over. To drive you need a valid licence and a roadworthy, registered and insured vehicle, approved by ARYV.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Settings, then Privacy, then Delete My Account. You can also email us. See Delete your account for what is removed and what is kept.
