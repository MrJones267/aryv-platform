Share the ride. Send the package.

One app, three ways to use it

Switch between them whenever you like. You only verify your documents if you want to drive or deliver.

Passengers

Find a seat going your way

  • Search rides by where you are going and when.
  • Join part of a longer trip and pay only for your part of it.
  • No ride yet? Post a request and nearby drivers can see it.
  • See the price before you book.
Drivers

Fill your empty seats

  • Post a trip with your stops, seats and price per seat.
  • Accept or decline each booking request.
  • Check each passenger's pickup PIN before you set off.
  • Passengers pay you in cash; ARYV's commission is settled separately.
Senders and couriers

Deliver with someone already travelling

  • Describe the package, set your offer, and a courier accepts it.
  • Follow the package on the map while it is on its way.
  • The recipient gets a delivery code; the courier needs it to finish.
  • Something went wrong? Report it and ARYV reviews the delivery.

How a ride works

From search to drop-off.

  1. Search or postPassengers search for a ride; drivers post the trip they are making.
  2. Request a seatThe driver sees your request and accepts or declines it.
  3. Meet and checkRead your pickup PIN to the driver so you both know it is the right car.
  4. Travel and payPay the driver in cash, then rate each other when the ride ends.

Built so strangers can trust each other

Sharing a car or handing over a package only works when both sides know who they are dealing with.

Verified drivers and couriers

Nobody can offer a ride or carry a package until ARYV staff have reviewed their identity and vehicle documents.

Pickup PIN

Each booking has a PIN that only the passenger sees. The driver checks it at pickup.

A photo from today

Passenger and driver each take a face photo for the trip, shown to the other once the booking is confirmed and deleted a day later.

Emergency alert

An SOS button shares your location with your emergency contacts and with ARYV's on-call staff.

Delivery code

A package is only marked delivered when the courier enters the code the recipient was sent.

Block and report

Block anyone you do not want to deal with again, and report behaviour for ARYV to review.
