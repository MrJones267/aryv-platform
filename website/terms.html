1. What ARYV is

ARYV is a platform that puts people in touch: passengers with drivers who are making a trip, and people sending a package with couriers who can carry it. ARYV does not provide transport or delivery itself. Drivers and couriers are independent users of the platform, not employees or agents of ARYV, and the trip or delivery is an arrangement between the users who agree to it.

2. Your account

  • You must be at least 18 years old.
  • Give accurate, current information, and keep it up to date.
  • Keep your password safe. You are responsible for what happens under your account.
  • Tell us straight away if you think someone else has used your account.
  • One person, one account. Do not share your account or let someone else drive or deliver under it.

3. Rides

  • To offer rides you must hold a valid driving licence, have a roadworthy and properly registered and insured vehicle, and have your documents approved by ARYV.
  • Every booking is a request until the driver accepts it. Drivers may decline requests.
  • The driver sets the price per seat. The price you are shown when you book is the price you pay.
  • Either side may cancel a booking before the ride. Please cancel as early as you can.
  • Drivers must follow the road traffic laws and carry no more passengers than the vehicle is licensed for.
  • After a ride, passengers and drivers may rate each other. Ratings must be honest.

4. Package delivery

  • The sender describes the package and names the price. A courier who accepts agrees to deliver it for that price.
  • Describe the package accurately. Do not send anything illegal, dangerous, stolen, perishable without saying so, or anything a courier could not lawfully carry. Do not send cash or items you cannot afford to lose.
  • When the courier collects the package, the recipient is sent a delivery code. The delivery is complete when the courier enters that code. Only give the code to the courier once you have the package.
  • Delivery times are estimates, not guarantees.
  • ARYV does not insure packages. A package is carried at the risk of the sender and the courier.
  • If something goes wrong, either side can report a problem with the delivery in the app. ARYV will review what happened and record an outcome, which may change the commission owed. ARYV's review does not replace any legal rights the sender and courier have against each other.

5. Payment and commission

  • Rides and deliveries are paid in cash, directly between users: the passenger pays the driver, and the recipient or sender pays the courier. ARYV does not hold or transfer this money.
  • Both sides confirm a cash payment in the app so each has a record of it.
  • ARYV charges drivers and couriers a commission on what they earn through the platform. The app shows the amount owed.
  • Drivers and couriers must settle commission when asked. An account that owes more than its limit cannot take new cash work until it is settled.
  • Promotional discounts are applied when you book and cannot be exchanged for cash.

7. Safety

ARYV checks drivers' and couriers' documents and provides safety tools such as the pickup PIN, trip photos, emergency alerts, blocking and reporting. These reduce risk; they do not remove it. Use your own judgment about who you travel with and what you carry. In an emergency, contact the emergency services first.
