Last updated: 10 November 2026

Location. ARYV asks for precise location and uses it while the app is open and you are using a location feature.

There is one case where location is shared while the app is not on screen: a courier carrying a package. From pickup until delivery, the courier's position is sent to us and shown to the package's sender. Android shows a notification for as long as this is happening, and it stops when the delivery ends.

If you raise an emergency alert, your location is shared with your emergency contacts and ARYV staff until the alert is resolved.

Camera and microphone. The camera is used to take profile and trip photos, photograph verification documents, and scan delivery codes. The microphone is used only during an in-app call. We do not record calls.

1.3 What we do not collect

We do not collect your phone's contact list, your browsing outside the app, card or bank details, biometric identifiers, or data about your health, religion, politics or sexuality. The app contains no advertising and no third-party advertising trackers.

2. How we use your information

  • To provide the service: matching, booking, navigation and delivery.
  • To keep users safe: verifying drivers and couriers, responding to emergency alerts, and looking into reports and disputes.
  • To tell you about your trips, deliveries and account.
  • To detect and prevent fraud and abuse.
  • To meet legal, tax and regulatory obligations.
  • To improve the app, using aggregated figures.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for advertising.

Automated decisions. Ride matching and suggested prices are worked out automatically. Decisions that seriously affect you, such as approving or refusing a driver or courier application, suspending an account, or resolving a dispute, are made by a person.

3. Who we share it with

Other users, as far as the trip or delivery needs. A driver sees a passenger's name, photo and rating, and their pickup and drop-off points. A passenger sees the driver's name, photo, rating and vehicle. Phone numbers are shared between the two sides of a booking or an accepted delivery. A courier sees a package's pickup and drop-off contacts only after accepting it. Your identity documents are never shown to another user.

6. Your rights and your choices

You can, at any time:

  • See the personal data we hold about you, and get a copy of it.
  • Correct anything inaccurate, from the app's profile screens.
  • Delete your account and personal data, in the app or by writing to us. See Delete your account.
  • Object to or restrict how we use your data, in certain circumstances.
  • Turn off location, camera, microphone or notification access in your phone's settings. Some features will not work without them.
  • Complain to your data protection authority.

We rely on these legal grounds: carrying out our contract with you (providing the service), legal obligations (verifying drivers, keeping tax records), our legitimate interests (safety, preventing fraud, improving the app), and your consent where we ask for it.

6.1 What deleting your account does

Deleting your account erases your personal data: your name, email address, phone number, profile photo, date of birth, address, emergency contacts, verification documents and settings.

Completed trips, deliveries and payment records are kept without your identity. They cannot be removed entirely, because they are also the other person's record of the trip, and because we must keep financial records. Deletion is permanent.

7. Security

Passwords are stored as one-way hashes. Traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted. Sign-in tokens are kept in your phone's secure storage and are cancelled when you sign out or change your password. Verification documents are not public: they are shown only to the person who submitted them and to authorised reviewers. Access to our systems is limited to authorised staff, and staff actions are logged.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal data, we will tell you and the relevant authority as the law requires.

8. Children

ARYV is not for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes

We will post any change to this policy here and update the date at the top. For significant changes we will tell you in the app or by email before they take effect.
